# Offers lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Real pain in a market that is easy to find.** Pelvic floor problems, core separation and lost strength are felt daily, and postpartum mums gather where they can be reached (Instagram, mum groups).
- **A vivid dream outcome.** "Get my strength and body back" is something people want badly.
- **A credible story,** and the planned pre-sale tests real money, not opinions.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is near zero.** There are no results, no testimonials, no stated qualification and no named physio behind a program that targets diastasis and pelvic floor, both clinical areas. Buyers will not believe it works, and one safety problem could end the business.
2. **Effort works against the buyer.** 45 minutes a day, through YouTube links and a bot, asks a lot of a time-poor, sleep-deprived mum and gives her little support.
3. **A commodity at AUD 19.99, and the free version eats it.** A free 5-week program comes close to the 8-week paid one, and nothing in the stack makes saying no feel stupid. Being cheap is the only difference.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who designed the program, and has a women's health physio reviewed it?
- What result can a mum see by week 2, and how will she know?
- Would 15 to 20 minutes deliver most of the outcome?
- What does the paid 8 weeks give that the free 5 weeks does not?
- How many of the 10 sign-ups are not friends?
- What would you add to charge double and still be the obvious choice?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. A qualified women's health physio reviews the program and is named on it, with a safety screen (such as medical clearance at 6 weeks).
2. The daily session defaults to about 15 to 20 minutes, with longer sessions optional.
3. The free program delivers a quick win, not most of the result; the paid offer is stacked with fixes for real obstacles (check-ins, a diastasis self-check, community) and an honest guarantee.
4. The pass bar is met: 3+ paid pre-orders by 20 November 2026 from buyers who are not friends, plus interview evidence on how much time they really have.
