## The stack

- **Core:** "Mama Fit Core Comeback": 8 weeks of 15 to 20 minute sessions, 4 days a week, for pelvic floor, tummy gap, core, legs and arms. Reviewed by a named women's health physio.
- **Free:** 1-week quick start (4 sessions).
- **Bonuses:** diastasis self-check video; private group of mums starting the same week; weekly check-in from the founder.
- **Guarantee:** 14 days, full refund, no questions. Costed at a 10% claim rate ($2.00 a sale) in `numbers.json`.
- **Urgency (true):** founding group starts 1 December 2026, capped at 30 because the founder does every check-in herself.
- **Price:** AUD 19.99 one-off for now (see `pricing.md`).

## What the re-test showed

- **Fixed:** the time objection. It went from the top reason in v1 to almost gone. Every buyer who said yes named the short sessions.
- **Still blocking:** buyers who already pay for an app (0 of 3), class-goers who need in-person accountability (0 of 3), physio-first buyers who want a **named** physio (0 of 3), and free YouTube users (0 of 5).
- **Next fixes:** name the physio with her credentials, add a monthly live session or meet-up for class-goers, and get one trusted creator or physio to recommend it.
- **Watch:** buyers who said yes still worry they'll drop off after 2 to 3 weeks. The weekly check-in has to work.

Claims: avoid promising to "fix" or "close" diastasis. Australian Consumer Law and the TGA advertising rules apply to health claims. Not legal advice.
