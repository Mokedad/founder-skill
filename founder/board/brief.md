# Mama Fit

- What it is: a web app (native app later) with a home strength program for new mums: 8 workouts, 3 sets of 10, about 45 minutes a day, targeting pelvic floor, diastasis recti recovery, core, legs and arms. No diet or nutrition content.
- Who it is for: mums roughly 6 weeks to 18 months postpartum who want to rebuild strength and get back to their pre-pregnancy body, and who are short on time.
- What it sells, at what price: a free 5-week program (lead magnet) and a paid 8-week program at AUD 19.99, one-off purchase (in-app purchase).
- Where and how: online, Australia first. Customers find it through the founder's Instagram, which links to the web app. Workouts are shown as videos (YouTube links) and the founder wants delivery automated (bots/AI guide the user through the program) so she is not personally involved day to day.
- Budget and constraints: budget not stated. Founder is a mum of three who has been through postpartum recovery herself; friends already ask her about her fitness journey. She is not stated to hold a fitness or physio qualification. 10 email sign-ups so far. A validation test is planned: 6-8 interviews plus a $19.99 pre-sale, pass bar 3+ paid pre-orders by 20 November 2026.
