# Pricing: Mama Fit Core Comeback

## The price

**Launch at AUD 19.99 for the founding group of 30, then test AUD 29.99 as the standard price.**

- **Buyers (simulated, offer v2):** acceptable range AUD 7 to 49. "Bargain" median $15, "getting expensive" median $40 (`pricing-curve.md`). Both $19.99 and $29.99 sit comfortably inside it. These are simulated answers, so use them to choose what to test, not as proof.
- **Competitors:** 8 weeks of a paid postpartum program costs AUD 44 (KIC, 2 months) to AUD 199 (Find Your Balance, one-off), median about AUD 99 (`competitors.md`). Most are subscriptions. Mama Fit at $19.99 or $29.99 one-off is the cheapest paid option and the only low-cost one with no subscription. Free YouTube programs cost $0.
- **Margin (CFO tool):** $19.99 gives year 1 profit of about $464, a 32% margin. $29.99 gives about $2,459, 55%. $24.99 gives about $1,462, 45%. Price is the biggest lever the founder controls.
- **Why not stay at $19.99:** a lower price doesn't win buyers who would otherwise pay $99. In the panel, people said no because of habit and trust, not price. Only 1 of 20 in panel v1 and 0 in panel v2 gave price as the reason.

## The ladder

| Rung | What | Price | Reason to step up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1-week quick start, 4 sessions | $0 | Try it in nap time |
| Core Comeback | 8 weeks, physio-reviewed, group, weekly check-in | $19.99 founding / $29.99 standard | The full program and the support |
| Comeback Plus (later) | Core + monthly live group session + one video form check from the founder | test $59 | For class-goers and mums who want accountability; panel v2 class-goers asked for something live |

Keep it one-off, no subscription. Billing complaints (auto-renewal, charges after cancelling) are the top complaint about competitors (11 reviews). "Pay once" is part of the gap.

## Opening offer

"Founding group price AUD 19.99 for the first 30 mums starting 1 December 2026." This is true: the cap exists because the founder does every check-in. After the founding group, the standard price is $29.99. Don't advertise a "was $29.99" price until $29.99 has actually been charged (no fake anchors).

## What to test with real buyers

1. **Pre-sale (now to 20 November):** sell the founding group at $19.99. Pass bar from the validation plan: 3+ paid pre-orders from people who aren't friends.
2. **Second cohort (January 2027):** split the page traffic. Half see $24.99, half see $29.99, for 2 weeks each. Keep the price with the higher revenue per visitor, not the higher number of buyers.

## Top price objections (verbatim, simulated buyers: research only, never use as testimonials)

- "Twenty bucks isn't the issue, the time is." (P013, v1)
- "I'd grab the free week during a night feed, but between that and all the free postpartum core stuff from the creators I already follow, I honestly don't think I'd need to pay for the full 8 weeks." (P010, v2)
- "I'm not paying for the next bit until I've actually finished it." (P010, v1)
