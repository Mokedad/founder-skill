# Brand: the postpartum core program that fits in a nap

Positioning (from `marketing.md`): for mums short on time, the **postpartum core program that fits in a nap**. 15 to 20 minutes. Physio-reviewed. Pay once.
Market: Australia first. Checks run on 6 November 2026.

This is not legal advice. I can only run public web searches. A trade mark lawyer or attorney confirms a name. Nothing is yours until you register it.

## The short answer

- **"Mama Fit" is risky.** Several fitness brands and apps for mums already use it or something very close (evidence below). It is also hard to protect, because it describes the product.
- **Recommended name: Nap Strong.** No fitness business or app found using it. It says the positioning out loud.
- **Tagline: "Stronger by the time they wake."**
- **Colours: Deep Teal #1F3B3A, Clay #A84F2C, Oat #FBF4EA.**

Why this matters:
- **Confusion.** A mum searching "Mama Fit" in the App Store or on Instagram will find other brands first.
- **Trade mark.** An existing "Mamafit®" in the same field could object if you file, or if you grow. IP Australia may also refuse "Mama Fit" as too descriptive for fitness for mums.
- **Search.** You would never own the search results.

Keep it for what it is good at: a warm phrase in captions ("mama, fit in 15"). Not the name on the app.

## 3. Shortlist of three

1. **Nap Strong** (recommended). It is the positioning in two words. Easy to say in a reel. Easy to spell.
2. **Napora.** Most protectable, because it is invented. But it costs more to explain.
3. **Wattle Core.** Says "Australian" and "core". Harder to protect and harder to grow overseas.

Keep "Mama Fit Core Comeback" out of the name. Call the product "the 8-week Core Program" or "Nap Strong: 8 Weeks".

## 4. Checks you must finish yourself

Do these before you print anything or pay for a logo.

### Trade mark (Australia)
- Search here: [IP Australia trade mark search](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/quick).
- Search each name, plus close spellings ("NapStrong", "Nap Strong", "Napstrong"; "Mama Fit", "Mamafit", "Fit Mama").
- Classes to check:
  - **Class 41**: fitness instruction, online exercise classes, training programs.
  - **Class 9**: downloadable apps, downloadable workout videos.
  - **Class 44**: only if you describe it as health or physiotherapy services.
- If you sell overseas later: [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/), [UKIPO](https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext), [USPTO](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/).
- Also check the business name register: [ASIC business name search](https://connectonline.asic.gov.au/RegistrySearch/faces/landing/SearchRegisters.jspx).
- A trade mark attorney can do a clearance search for a few hundred dollars. Worth it before you file.

### Domains
- What I checked (Shopify domain tool, 6 Nov 2026, point in time only):
  - napstrong.com: **taken**.
  - getnapstrong.com: **available** at the time (USD 17).
  - mamafit.com, napora.com, wattlecore.com: **taken**.
- **Not verified:** any .com.au. The tool did not return a result, and direct registry lookups were blocked here.
- Check these yourself: napstrong.com.au (first choice), getnapstrong.com, napstrong.app.
- Look-up links: [auDA .au WHOIS](https://whois.auda.org.au/), [ICANN lookup for .com](https://lookup.icann.org/).
- To hold a .com.au you need an Australian connection (an ABN works). Register promptly; availability can change.

### Handles
Check each one. I did not verify any.
- Instagram: instagram.com/napstrong (then @napstrong.au, @napstrongmums)
- TikTok: tiktok.com/@napstrong
- YouTube: youtube.com/@napstrong

### Confusion with competitors

- Drop "Core Comeback" as a program name (four other creators use it).

## 5. Promise and tagline

**The one-line promise:** Every session fits in a nap, every exercise has a safe easier option, and you pay once.

**Tagline, three options:**
1. **"Stronger by the time they wake."** (recommended; no existing use found)
2. "Your core, one nap at a time."
3. "Fifteen minutes. Physio-reviewed. Paid once."

Avoid "fix", "close your gap", "flat tummy" or "lose weight". These are health claims under Australian Consumer Law and TGA rules.

## 6. Voice

Three adjectives.

| Adjective | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Calm** | Short sentences. Say what to do next. "Missed a day? Pick up where you left off." | Hype or guilt. "No excuses!" "Bounce back!" |
| **Honest** | Say who checked it and what it can't do. "See a physio if it hurts or you leak more." | Promise results. "Fix your diastasis in 8 weeks." |
| **Mum-to-mum** | Talk like a friend who has done it three times. "I did this one with a baby on the mat." | Clinical jargon or drill-sergeant tone. "Engage your transversus abdominis, ladies." |

**Before** (from `pitch.md`):
> Mama Fit Core Comeback: an 8-week home program for mums after birth, made by a mum of three and reviewed by a women's health physio.

**After:**
> Nap Strong is 8 weeks of core work for after birth. Each session takes 15 minutes. A mum of three made it. A women's health physio checked every move.

### Colours

| Clay | #A84F2C | Buttons, links, one highlight per screen. Warm and human. |

Contrast (WCAG 2.1, computed from relative luminance):

| Pair | Ratio | AA body text (4.5:1) | AA large text (3:1) |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Deep Teal on Oat | 11.02:1 | Pass | Pass |
| Deep Teal on white | 12.03:1 | Pass | Pass |
| White on Clay (buttons) | 5.50:1 | Pass | Pass |
| Clay on Oat (links) | 5.03:1 | Pass | Pass |
| Deep Teal on Clay | 2.19:1 | **Fail** | **Fail**. Never use. |

A lighter clay (#C8643B) only reaches 3.60:1 on Oat. Use it for shapes and illustration, not text.

These colours are not taken from any competitor in `competitors.md`. Competitor palettes were not audited; glance at their app icons before you finalise.

### Type
- **Display: Fraunces** (soft serif, warm). Free, SIL Open Font License. [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces).
- **Text: DM Sans** (plain, readable on a phone at night). Free, SIL Open Font License. [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans).
- Both are free for commercial use, apps and logos.

### Logo brief
- **Must say:** short, safe, strong. Night-time calm, not gym.
- **Idea to try:** a crescent moon resting on a short bar, or a lowercase "n" shaped like a sleeping curve. Wordmark "Nap Strong" in Fraunces.
- **Must work:** as a **32px app icon** (one shape, no text, Oat on Deep Teal), an Instagram profile circle, a web header, a printed A5 physio flyer, one colour.
- **Avoid:** pregnant silhouettes, mother-and-baby outlines (every competitor uses them), dumbbells, flexed arms, pink, anything like a competitor's mark.
- If you have an image tool, I can make concepts. They are concepts only, not a finished logo. A designer should draw the final one, and you should own the files.

### The first five touchpoints

1. **Homepage / Instagram bio.** Headline: "Stronger by the time they wake." Line two: "15-minute postpartum core sessions. Physio-reviewed. Pay once, AUD 19.99." One Clay button: "Start the free week". Name the physio near the top.
2. **Packaging (the app's first screen).** "Welcome. Today's session is 15 minutes. Put the baby down, roll out a towel, press play." Show the easier option button straight away.
3. **Receipt / confirmation email.** Subject: "You're in. No subscription, nothing to cancel." Say the price once, the 14-day refund, and the start date (1 December 2026).
4. **First social post.** A reel filmed during a real nap: timer on screen, one session, baby monitor in shot. Caption: "15 minutes. That's the whole thing." No before/after photos.
5. **Reply to the first complaint.** "I'm sorry, that's not good enough. I've refunded you in full, no need to explain. If you're happy to tell me what went wrong, I'll fix it for the next mum." Reply within a day. Refund first, ask second.
