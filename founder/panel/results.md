## What would flip a no

- If my GP or physio pointed me to it for my diastasis because it does more than my current app's postpartum plan, with shorter 20-minute sessions I could actually finish during a nap.
- Shorter 15-20 minute sessions I could do after 6pm when my partner is home, plus a physio's backing on the pelvic floor and diastasis work so it feels safer than random YouTube videos.
- A group element, like a weekly live session or a small mums' group doing the 8 weeks together with check-ins, and shorter 20-minute sessions.
- If a women's health physio designed or endorsed the 8-week program, or my physio said it was safe and useful for me after I finished the free weeks.
- If the sessions were 10-15 minutes I could do during a nap, and I actually finished the free 5 weeks and noticed less leaking, I'd pay the $19.99.
- If it was clearly made with a pelvic floor physio and the sessions were 15 to 20 minutes, and it did more for my tummy gap than my current app, I'd cancel that and switch to this.
- Short 15-20 minute sessions plus some kind of group or live check-in with other mums so there's accountability, ideally with a women's health physio involved.
- A creator I already follow recommending it, plus shorter 15-20 minute sessions I could finish in one nap.
- Short 10-15 minute sessions I can do while the baby naps, plus proof it does the pelvic floor and tummy gap work better than my current app, like a women's health physio behind it.
- A mum-fitness creator I already follow showing her results from the paid 8 weeks, with shorter 15-20 minute sessions I could fit into nap time.
- Short 10 to 15 minute sessions I can finish in one nap, and actually sticking with the free 5 weeks and feeling my core and pelvic floor improve.
- If a women's health physio designed or signed off the program and it showed how to modify exercises for diastasis and pelvic floor issues, or my physio recommended it.
