## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $150 a month (Web app / course platform (estimate) $60, Video hosting, private (estimate) $25, Email tool (estimate) $20, Fitness professional liability insurance (estimate) $35, Domain, misc software (estimate) $10).

- **Break-even: 1 one paid 8-week program sales a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (1 a day): **37%** of every sale, after every cost.
