# Mama Fit Core Comeback: one page

**Problem:** new mums want their core, pelvic floor and strength back but "can't find 45 minutes with a baby" and "don't know what's safe for my tummy gap". Free YouTube gives no guidance. Apps charge AUD 44 to 199 for 8 weeks, mostly as subscriptions people complain about.

**Customer:** Australian mums 6 weeks to 18 months after birth, cleared to exercise. Best early fit: mums back at work, and mums who start strong then drop off.

**Offer and price:** 8 weeks of 15 to 20 minute nap-time sessions, reviewed by a women's health physio, with an easier option for every move. Includes a diastasis self-check, a small group of mums starting together, and a weekly founder check-in. Free first week, then **AUD 19.99 one-off** for the founding group of 30, testing $29.99 after. 14-day refund.
