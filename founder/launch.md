# Launch: Mama Fit Core Comeback

## Step 1: test before you spend (already planned)

**The test:** a founding-group pre-sale at AUD 19.99, plus 6 to 8 Mom-Test interviews (see the Mama Fit Interview Kit).

- **Window:** Mon 9 Nov to Fri 20 Nov 2026.
- **Success line (written before running it):** **3 or more paid pre-orders from people who aren't friends**, and most interviewees describe the time and safety pain unprompted.
- **Compare with the panel:** offer v2 got 20% of simulated buyers. If real page visitors convert at well under 5%, trust the real buyers and go back to `/founder-offer` or `/founder-pricing`.
- **Spend nothing big before 20 November** except a phone-filmed free week and a simple payment link. The physio review (~$1,500) happens only after the pre-sale passes. Until then, the page says "physio review in progress", never "reviewed".

## Step 4: the first 30 days

**Track weekly:**

| Number | Target | Change something if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid founding mums | 30 by 30 Nov | Under 10 by 30 Nov |
| Free-week to paid | 10%+ | Under 10% |
| Cost per paid customer | Under $6 | Over $6: stop ads |
| Mums doing sessions in week 3 | 50%+ | Under 50%: fix check-ins |
| Refund requests | Under 10% | Over 10%: ask why, fix the cause |
| Pain or leaking reports | 0 | Any: physio reviews that exercise |

**Reviews:**
- **Day 7 (8 Dec):** Did everyone get in? Which session got skipped most? What did people ask?
- **Day 14 (15 Dec):** Who has gone quiet, and did a check-in bring them back?
- **Day 30 (31 Dec):** Completion, refunds and the real cost per buyer. Decide the January price ($24.99 vs $29.99 test) and whether to add the monthly live session.
