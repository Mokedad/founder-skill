# Marketing: Mama Fit Core Comeback

## Positioning

Three versions:

1. For **mums back at work or stretched for time**, who *"can't find 45 minutes with a baby"*, Mama Fit is the **postpartum core program that fits in a nap**: 15 to 20 minutes, physio-reviewed, paid once. Unlike subscription apps, there's nothing to cancel.
2. For mums who leak or have a tummy gap and *"don't know what's safe"*, Mama Fit is the physio-reviewed home program, unlike random free YouTube videos.
3. For Australian mums who want *"someone to notice if I skip"*, Mama Fit is the 8-week group comeback with a weekly check-in from a mum of three.

**Recommended: 1.** In panel v2, every buyer who said yes named the short sessions. The biggest buyer groups were "back at work" (40%) and "starts strong, drops off" (100%, thin). Competitors' top complaint is subscription billing (11 reviews, `competitors.md`). Use 2 and 3 as supporting messages.

## Ten hooks (each answers an objection)

1. "I couldn't find 45 minutes with a baby either. So I made it 15." (time)
2. "My core workout, done before the nap ended." (time, video)
3. "Reviewed by a women's health physio. Every exercise has an easier option." (safety)
4. "Missed three days? You pick up where you left off." (drop-off)
5. "Pay once. No subscription. No surprise renewals." (billing complaint)
6. "15 minutes. Pelvic floor, core, legs, arms. Floor only." (effort)
7. "Is it safe for your tummy gap? Here's how to check first." (safety, links to self-check)
8. "Who designed this? A mum of three, checked by a physio." (trust)
9. "Free YouTube is great until you don't know if you're doing it right." (free substitutes)
10. "A small group of mums starting the same week, and I check in every Friday." (accountability)

Rules: no before/after photos, no "fix your diastasis" or "lose weight" promises, no fake reviews, and disclose the gifted creator partnership. Health-claim rules apply (TGA and Australian Consumer Law). Not legal advice.

## Budget and numbers

- **Budget for the first 30 days: about $300** (launch ads in `numbers.json`): $100 ad test after the pre-sale, $100 printed flyers for physio clinics, $100 kept back for boosting the best-performing reel.
- **The most you can pay to win a customer:** each sale leaves $17.35 before ads ($19.99 minus $0.64 card fee minus $2.00 refund reserve). The panel's first-month purchase is 1 per buyer, with no repeat sales yet. So break-even is $17 per paid customer. **Aim for $6 or less**, which is what the CFO assumed. At $29.99 the ceiling rises to about $27.
- **Three numbers to watch weekly:**
  1. Free-week sign-ups → paid: below 10% means change the offer.
  2. Cost per paid customer: above $6 means stop ads and go organic.
  3. Founding mums still doing sessions in week 3: below 50% means fix the check-ins before selling the next group.
