## What it is
- Subscription core and pelvic-floor exercise program for pregnancy and postpartum, built with pelvic floor physical therapists; claims to be clinically proven to resolve diastasis recti (company claim, not verified). https://apps.apple.com/us/app/every-mother/id1369750567 , https://every-mother.com/diastasis-recti
- iOS (id 1369750567) and Android (package `com.everymother`). https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.everymother&hl=en_US

## Program structure ("EMbody" stages)
- **Prepare**: pregnancy, any stage.
- **Reclaim**: postpartum start; a 12-week program.
- **Surpass**: after Reclaim; maintenance, pitched at 10-20 min a day.
- Members can switch between stages at any time.
Sources: https://every-mother.com/faq/ , https://every-mother.com/empower/best-at-home-workout-for-moms

## Core user flow (as far as public pages show)
1. Join on the web or in the app, pick a plan (free trial offered on some plans; the membership policy refers to a trial "should a free trial be available"). https://every-mother.com/members/join , https://every-mother.com/membership-policy
2. Pick a stage (Prepare / Reclaim / Surpass).
3. Follow a guided "Path" of daily sessions; a short foundational exercise (10 min, per a company reply on Trustpilot) is recommended daily. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/every-mother.com
4. Enter diastasis measurements when the Path prompts you; progress pictures are also stored. https://every-mother.com/faq/
- Onboarding assessment: not confirmed from public pages in this run (unknown).

## Session length
- Full workouts: about 10 minutes to under 30 minutes (FAQ). https://every-mother.com/faq/
- Listing/third-party: 10-15 minute express sessions. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/every-mother/id1369750567
- Surpass: 10-20 min a day. https://every-mother.com/empower/best-at-home-workout-for-moms

## Tracking
- Diastasis measurement tracker plus progress pictures, prompted inside the Path; not accessible after cancelling. https://every-mother.com/faq/
