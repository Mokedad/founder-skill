# Every Mother: what users hate, what is missing, what is unsolved

Research for Mama Fit (postpartum program app for Australian mums: 15-20 min sessions, physio-reviewed, pay once). Run date 2026-11-07.

## Sample size, read this first

**4 verbatim review excerpts from 2 sources (Trustpilot 3, Google Play 1). 0 Apple App Store, 0 Reddit, 0 forums. None have a star rating or date.** Target was 100+ across 3+ sources; this run did not get close.

Why: this session's network refused every review and product host. Blocked by the proxy (curl) and by WebFetch: itunes.apple.com (the Apple RSS feed, all 40 requests for us/au/gb/ca pages 1-10), apps.apple.com, play.google.com, every-mother.com, trustpilot.com (incl. ch.), appfollow.io, hn.algolia.com, www.google.com. A request to create a network-allowed environment for these hosts was rejected by the environments service. The only working route was the WebSearch tool, which returns a model-written summary of results, not raw page snippets. Only sentences that appear as first-person review text or review titles in those results were kept, each linked to the review page the search attributed it to. They have NOT been checked against the live page. Treat them as leads, not evidence.

Everything below is therefore **thin**. `feedback.md` (generated by reviews.py) marks every theme thin (1 review, 1 source each). Nothing here supports a ranking.

## Search-summary signals (paraphrase, not quotes, not counted)

The search tool's summaries described these patterns in pages it read. They are not quotes and are not in reviews.csv; they tell you where to look once the hosts are reachable:
- Trustpilot: renewals charged after users believed they had cancelled; no renewal reminder; refund requests declined; one user charged a second annual fee while the first term was still active. Company replies cite its no-refund policy. Pages: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/every-mother.com , https://au.trustpilot.com/review/every-mother.com , https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/every-mother.com?page=5
- Every Mother's own policy says no refunds on memberships; cancelling only stops future renewals: https://every-mother.com/membership-policy
- Google Play: a user charged a day before the cancellation deadline (later refunded); a user asking for a 3- or 7-day trial: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.everymother&hl=en_US
- App Store: a user who believed they had cancelled in 2023 and was charged annual renewals 2024-2026: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/every-mother/id1369750567?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone
- Trustpilot: a reviewer whose DR got worse, and who found the help after a C-section lacking compared with after a vaginal birth: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/every-mother.com (page attribution uncertain)
- App Store: a reviewer saying some exercises were not appropriate for significant DR and the app doesn't replace in-person physio: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/every-mother/id1369750567?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone
- Video lag on a Kindle Fire (third-party site): https://every-mother.myprosandcons.com/

## 3. What is unsolved (leads only, 0 verbatim)

- **C-section mums.** Search signal only: one reviewer found C-section education/help lacking. 0 verbatim quotes.

## Positioning angles

1. **Pay once, no traps (recommended).**
   For Australian mums who are wary of being auto-billed for an app they stopped using, Mama Fit is a one-off payment with no renewal at all.
   Evidence: billing, 1 verbatim review across 1 source (thin), plus uncounted billing signals on 3 platforms and Every Mother's no-refund policy. Strongest lead and it matches Mama Fit's pay-once model, but confirm with a real sample first.
2. **Starts where your body actually is.**
   For mums who found "beginner" postpartum workouts too hard, Mama Fit starts with a genuinely gentle level and moves up only when you're ready.
   Evidence: difficulty fit, 1 review across 1 source (thin).
3. **Built for C-section recovery too.**
   For mums recovering from a caesarean who feel generic core programs ignore them, Mama Fit has a physio-reviewed C-section track.
   Evidence: 0 verbatim reviews; one search-summary signal. Weakest; needs research.

Do not use Every Mother's name in Mama Fit's name, ads or store listing. Reviewers' words are research, not testimonials.
