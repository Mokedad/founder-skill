# Summary

**Verdict: Not yet.** The money works if people buy, but buyer demand is below the bar: 4 of 20 simulated buyers buy (20%, bar 25%), and no real buyer has paid yet. **What has to change:** a named women's health physio on the program, and a real pre-sale that clears 3+ paid pre-orders from strangers by 20 November 2026 (`/founder-launch`). If it falls short, go back to `/founder-offer`.

**What it is:** Mama Fit Core Comeback is an 8-week home program of 15 to 20 minute sessions for Australian mums after birth, covering pelvic floor, tummy gap, core, legs and arms. It's made by a mum of three, reviewed by a women's health physio, AUD 19.99 one-off with a free first week.

**The three numbers (estimates, CFO tool):**
- Each sale leaves $11.35 after card fees, ads and refunds (57% contribution).
- Break-even is about 14 sales a month (fixed costs $150 a month).
- Year 1 operating profit is about $464 at $19.99, or about $2,459 at $29.99. The founder's time is not counted.

**Where the board and panel agree:** the original 45-minute, 5-free-weeks, no-physio version doesn't sell (panel v1: 0 of 20). Shorter sessions fixed the time objection (panel v2: 4 of 20). Safety and credibility need a named physio; all three board members and every "physio-first" buyer said so.

**Where they disagree:** the Monopoly lens passes. A low one-off price is easy for KIC or Sweat to copy, so the only moat is the founder's story, a physio partner and the community. Offers and Product would fund it if the conditions are met.

**Biggest risk:** trust in a clinical area (diastasis and pelvic floor) without a qualified designer. **Fix:** a physio reviews the program and is named on it before launch, plus a "stop and see a physio" path. Launch on 8 December instead of 1 December if the review isn't done.

**What the founder needs to start:** about $3,200 cash (physio ~$1,500, filming kit, setup, legal templates, launch ads), and the pre-sale test from 9 to 20 November.
